## 3. Structural lessons
1. **Tests that fake the trigger.** T7 B1: hosted tests posted the occlusion notification themselves, so the main path (a window opened with a radius already set) was never exercised. Only a probe on a real launch found it. The adversarial tester at xhigh later passed T7 on a locked console, still without a real window server. A UI feature whose tests drive the system trigger by hand needs one non-faked check; today that is accidental.
2. **Console locked all wave.** Swift verification was hosted XCTest only: no eye check, no composited capture, no real drag. T6 was "ship" with a visible 1x seam only because the critic wrote a bitmap probe. User eye checks (#363 matrix, T7 radius) stay open and are the true acceptance of two tasks.
3. **PR-body keywords are live.** #400's body said "T6 closes #388 / T7 closes #390"; GitHub closed both on merge; the lead reopened them. The T7 brief added "never write closes/fixes prose anywhere". verify-branch does not look at this (it checks only the commit subject and trailers).
4. **Stacked branches paid off, serial merges did not.** T7 was started on top of T6's commit while #404 was in CI (saved about 40 min of the critical path T1 -> T5 -> T6 -> T7); everywhere else strict up-to-date plus 21 min of CI per PR forced about 6 merge cycles with rebase integrators (T2, T4 rebases cost 2 integrator runs, 3.5k tokens, but 20+ min each of wall clock). #391 sat queued 2.4 h on a runner before an `update-branch` restarted it.
5. **Spec-heavy issues stay expensive.** T2 (#377): spec research widened the scope, the brief was rewritten, the PR is 2,000 lines. Fine, but it needed 4 lead decisions and still a trivial S7 blocker.
6. **Parallelism was not the limit; the lead's waiting was.** Lead span 8.5 h, 129 min active. About 12 "implementer is waiting on its own background work" idle notifications and 4 repeated reports ("That repeats ...") cost lead turns, not tokens of value.
7. **Minors filing.** 8 minors issues (#392-#394, #397-#399, #403, #405; #398 is a real Rust bug) for 8 tasks, 2-7 items each. Roughly half are doc/test polish. #392 (2 items on a docs PR) and #394 (3) are below what the one-round policy needs; #393 and #397 hold real items.
8. **Placeholder integrator report.** T0's integrator returned just "placeholder"; the lead verified by hand (commit, PR body, stamp). Integrator reports must carry commit hash, gate exit and verify exit.
9. **Lead decisions D1-D10 made without asking** were reasonable and recorded in the plan; none was overturned. D4/D5 held after research; D2's fallback was never needed (critic: splits always take the focused pane's profile).
