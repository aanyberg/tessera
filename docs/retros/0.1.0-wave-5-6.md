## 3. Five biggest time sinks
1. **Silent stalls nobody noticed (about 1,300 task-minutes).** `impl-84` last wrote at 22:39 ("gate running in the background; waiting for its completion notification"); the lead asked at 05:04 and found the gate had hung 6.5 h on the wgpu deadlock (#137). `impl-44` last wrote at 06:26 ("Still running; I'll continue when its completion notification arrives") and spoke next at 15:23, 536 min later, because the user asked "What's the status?". `impl-101` was silent from 09:50 to 16:07 (377 min), the same pattern. The lead's own transcript has no event between 12:08 and 15:22 (194 min) with three tasks open; machine sleep is possible there, but impl-44 and impl-101 were silent while the lead was active, and the lead's 10:00 and 10:22 summaries still said "impl-44 is fixing the bash defects". Totals: #44 741 min, #84 517, #101 454, against a 148 min median. Cause for #44 and #101 not established (last event is a background gate or `app-test`); #156 now bounds tests and CI steps, not a teammate's background command or the lead's attention.
2. **Rounds that follow new code, and tasks that did not converge (845k tokens, 223 agent-minutes).** #100: 5 critic runs (238k) and 269 min; its round-1 fixes added a config-directory poll thread and lazy config creation, which caused the two round-2 majors. #86: 3 critics, 2 adversarial, 250 min, because the provisional rules broke on real zsh. #44: 2 critic rounds (the second found 3 more blockers), a failed adversarial run, bash dropped. #23: 3 critics and 2 adversarial. #152 addresses the rounds; it does not address the lead's rule decisions that failed (below).
3. **One loaded machine: cold builds, load, disk.** Five implementers plus reviewers: load average sampled at 39; integrator-21's 20 improvised nextest processes (858 s test, `impl-83` stall); review scratch builds of about 35 GB each (#21 and #83 adversarial) left 3.5 GiB free at 05:31, and 60 GB of scratch was still there at 07:53; `impl-84` hit ENOSPC. Every reviewer builds a `git archive` copy with an empty `target/`: critics average 14 min, adversarial testers 26 min, with the build share unmeasured.
4. **Adversarial runs sized to a cap they exceed.** 15 of 24 cycle-1 runs used more than 80 turns (median 83, max 124) before #154 set `maxTurns: 80`. After it, #29 and #44 stopped at the limit with no report; the lead resumed each by message (the run ends "partial result", about 5 min and several lead turns each). The adversarial run is also the longest serial stage after round 1: critic 14 + fixes + adversarial 26 + verify + integrate 3 + CI 9.
5. **The lead as a serial bottleneck and lead-decision rework.** 29 PRs pushed, watched and merged one at a time; 1040 turns, 575k tokens, 480M cache reads (about 460k tokens of context re-read per turn). 266 idle notifications arrived and about 166 of 529 lead text messages are acknowledgements that needed no action ("Nothing new", "still waiting", "repeats its report"), 77 of them between 03:00 and 07:59. Lead decisions that were reversed or overtaken: #88 (padding style), #86 (provisional reflow rules), #75 (option B, overtaken by option C then confirmed by the critic's measurement), #8 (DECCOLM resize policy D2'), the #29/#100 merge order (reversed at 06:48), #104's bar.

## 4. Structural lessons
- Parallelism is limited by the machine, not by slots. Five in flight plus reviewers meant load 39 and a full disk.
- Upstream/downstream pairs and function-level ownership in the briefs worked: no critic or adversarial finding was a collision between tasks. The exception shape was semantic: #8 merged #107's in-place reset and missed clearing `origin` and `alt_saved` (found by the implementer); #29 had to be adapted to #100.
- Pass criteria: the adversarial tester reproduced user-visible data loss and labelled it minor (#100 HFS+); a racy test passed (#74). #152's "flaky test is a BLOCKER" covers the second; the first needs a user-visible-loss clause.
- Briefs without a spec-researcher repeatedly carried wrong behaviour from xterm or POSIX (#88, #93/#94, #75, #8).
- The local pipeline is blind to CI-only surfaces (the fuzz workflow, a slower Swift type-check).
- Trailers: every branch fails `verify-branch` on trailers by design, so a real failure on that line is hidden by an expected one.

## 5. Proposals
Each cites its evidence row. Nothing here edits an agent or the runbook.

| # | change | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Heartbeat.** A teammate that starts a background gate, `app-test` or load run polls it with a bounded wait (at most 300 s per poll) and sends a one-line status every 20 min; the lead writes the last-event time per teammate in every status summary and messages any teammate silent over 30 min. Schedule a recurring lead check so a wait on CI cannot run past 20 min unobserved. | Sink 1: 536, 377, 384 min silences; lead summaries contradicting the transcripts |
| P2 | **Concurrency 3, load-aware.** Run up to 3 tasks; start a 4th only when the 1-minute load average is below the core count and `df` shows over 40 GiB free. No improvised load runs: `verify-branch.sh --parallel` only. | Sink 3: load 39; 858 s test; ENOSPC; integrator-21 improvised 20 runs |
| P3 | **Reviewer build reuse.** Brief critics and adversarial testers to start from an APFS clone of the implementer's warm target dir (`cp -c -R <wt>/target <scratch>/target`), delete it after the verdict, and record the time of the first test run in the report so the saving can be measured. | Sink 3: 35 GB scratch each, 14 and 26 min average, lead's estimate of 5-15 min cold; unmeasured |
| P4 | **Scratch cleanup after each merge.** The lead removes `<scratchpad>/*-<issue>` and the worktree and its `target/` as part of "Resync". | Disk at 3.5 GiB free at 05:31; 60 GB scratch at 07:53 |
| P5 | **Adversarial cap and report first.** `maxTurns` 120 (median run is 83); add: "write the report file by turn 60 and update it as you go; the report is the deliverable". Keep opus and xhigh for risky changes. | Sink 4: 15 of 24 over 80 turns; #29 and #44 ended without a report |
| P6 | **Integrator skips the gate when nothing changed.** If, after rebasing, `git diff <verified-commit> HEAD` is empty (same tree) and `origin/main` has not moved since verification, reuse the verified commit's gate stamp and skip the re-run; any difference runs the gate once. | 29 integrator runs each re-ran gate and verify, 3 min span and 140k tokens in total, int-to-merge median about 14 min (CI 7-10). How often the tree was identical is not measured; try it for a wave and count |
| P7 | **Trailers.** Put the lead's `Co-Authored-By` and `Claude-Session` lines in the implementer brief and pass `--coauthor` to `verify-branch.sh`; the integrator never writes its own session id. | 33 trailer messages; #73's wrong session id |
| P8 | **Adversarial severity.** Use BLOCKER and MINOR in `adversarial-tester`, with "user-visible data loss, a flaky test and a hang are BLOCKERS". | #100 HFS+ loss called minor; #74 racy test |
| P9 | **Researcher before brief** for any issue whose behaviour is defined by xterm or another terminal (not only spec-heavy ones). | #88, #93/#94, #75, #8 |
| P10 | **Lead replies to idle notifications only when they carry a new SHA, report or blocker.** | Sink 5: about 166 of 529 messages; about 460k cached tokens per turn |
| P11 | **Pre-merge dispatch for new workflows.** The release-engineer runs a new workflow once from the branch before the lead merges. | #14 and #153 |
| P12 | **Revisit `code-critic` on sonnet next wave.** Five runs, no misses seen, one false escalation (#104). | 2a |

### What not to change
- Round 1 as one strict `rust-critic`/`swift-critic` at opus and high: 13 of 29 first rounds found blockers, including performance and design problems nobody else found.
- The adversarial run for product code, opus, xhigh when risky: 6 of 24 first runs reproduced a blocker no critic found; cycle 2 passed 5 of 5.
- A scoped round 2 only when the fix added behaviour (#152): round 2 found its blockers in exactly such code; round 3 found none in 3 runs.
- Spec-researchers: 9 runs, 435k tokens, no correctness blocker in the researched areas after the fixtures.
- Integrator on sonnet and medium and `verify-branch.sh` as a script: 1.4% of tokens.
- Function-level ownership and upstream/downstream pairs in the briefs: no collision findings.
- Do not go back to 5 concurrent tasks until P2, P3 and P4 are measured.
